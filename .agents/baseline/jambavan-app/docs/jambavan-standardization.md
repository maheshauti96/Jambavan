# JAMBAVAN standard operating language

## Product position

**JAMBAVAN is a simple execution and accountability workspace for coaching institutes.**

It turns meeting decisions into owned work, keeps responsibilities visible, and gives leadership a clear view across departments.

## Task vocabulary

- **Task Owner:** the person responsible for the final result and the only person who completes the parent task.
- **Contributor:** a person responsible for one part of a shared task. Contributors report their own completion.
- **Task Family:** Squirrel, Angad, Sugriv, or HANUMAN.
- **Task Source:** Manual task, MOM task, Event task, Project responsibility, or another approved source.
- **Completion Evidence:** proof attached to show that work was completed.
- **Task Guidance:** reference material attached to help someone understand the work.
- **Acknowledgment:** confirmation that a person has seen or accepted an item.

## Task families

| Family | Use it for | Assignment model |
|---|---|---|
| Squirrel | Small, focused work with a short finish line | One Task Owner |
| Angad | A larger individual deliverable | One Task Owner, optional subtasks |
| Sugriv | Coordinated team responsibility | One Task Owner plus Contributors |
| HANUMAN | Strategic work that may run for months or a year | Owner, Contributors, updates, milestones |

## Official meeting workflow

Use **MOM Directory** only:

1. Attendance
2. Meeting date, department, Meeting Head, and MOM Secretary
3. Written MOM and decisions
4. Explicit quick-task rows with department and person assignment
5. Optional next-meeting date
6. Save and lock the written MOM

The written MOM is immutable after saving. Only authorized leaders may add task rows later.

## Employee profile language

- **Permanent profile:** name, photograph, contact, joining date, position, department, reporting manager, work schedule, and weekly off.
- **Availability:** Available, On leave, or Sick.
- **Attendance Today:** Present, Late, Absent, or On leave.
- **Leave record:** dates, reason, approval state, and approving manager.

## Navigation groups

- **Workspace:** My Feed, Department, Dashboard
- **Execution:** MOM Directory, Event Planner, Projects
- **Communication:** Yearly Calendar, Notices & Feedback
- **People & Control:** People, My Profile, Access Matrix, Audit Log

## Lifecycle terms

Use **Archived** for tasks, **Notice Archive** for expired notices, and **Deactivated** for employees who should lose access while historical records remain intact. Avoid permanently deleting business records during normal operations.
