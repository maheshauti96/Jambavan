# JAMBAVAN design brief

## Direction selection

No visual style was specified, so three deliberately distinct directions were considered:

- **Monsoon Ledger** — warm paper-like operations dashboard with indigo ink, saffron highlights, and meeting-notebook cues. Probability: 0.07
- **Coaching Control Room** — high-contrast dark command center with electric lime status signals and compact data density. Probability: 0.06
- **Quiet Momentum** — calm, pale mint workspace with soft coral emphasis and spacious, approachable task cards. Probability: 0.08

**Committed direction: Monsoon Ledger.** It fits a coaching institute because it feels like a reliable daily execution notebook rather than heavy enterprise project-management software, while still giving heads a clear control-room view.

## Expanded direction

- **Design movement:** editorial operations tool with Indian stationery cues, modernized for web.
- **Core principles:** make the next action obvious, keep accountability visible, use hierarchy without intimidation, reduce clicks.
- **Color philosophy:** deep ink indigo for structure, turmeric/saffron for active work, leafy green for completion, warm parchment for the canvas, coral only for overdue/high-attention states.
- **Layout paradigm:** fixed slim left rail on desktop, compact top header, wide content canvas, stacked cards with strong whitespace; on mobile the rail collapses to a bottom nav.
- **Signature elements:** JAMBAVAN mark, “today’s execution” greeting, a progress ribbon, chapter-like section labels, meeting action point cards.
- **Interaction philosophy:** optimistic, direct, friendly; task creation should feel like writing one clear line in a shared notebook.
- **Animation:** short ease-out transitions for cards and progress, no decorative motion that distracts from work.
- **Typography system:** Plus Jakarta Sans for UI and headings, IBM Plex Mono for small metadata/labels.
- **Brand essence:** steady strength, dependable follow-through, calm accountability.
- **Brand voice:** concise, encouraging, action-oriented; use coaching-institute language such as batches, faculty, operations, and MOM.
- **Wordmark/logo:** bold J-shaped pillar/leaf symbol suggesting a mentor's staff and a forward arrow, paired with the JAMBAVAN wordmark.
- **Signature brand color:** deep ink indigo `#1F2A56`, with saffron `#F2A93B` as the action color.

## Visual refresh: photo-first people

- Use real-looking profile photos wherever a person appears: task owners, assistants, department heads, meeting participants, the active user, and people cards.
- Keep a neutral initials fallback only while a required photo is unavailable; the product direction is that member profiles cannot be activated without a photo.
- Increase color through department accents, soft card gradients, colorful metric cards, and photo chips while preserving the calm Monsoon Ledger structure.
